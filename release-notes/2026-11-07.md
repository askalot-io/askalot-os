# Askalot Platform Release — 2026-11-07

Askalot now speaks Spanish, French, German and Hungarian, and so can your surveys.

## Highlights

- **The whole product in your language.** Choose English, Spanish, French, German or Hungarian in Profile Settings, and every page follows: navigation, lists, buttons, refusals and notices. An organization admin can set a default language for members who have not chosen one. Changing your language takes effect straight away, with no reload needed.
- **Field one questionnaire in several languages.** Add translations of a questionnaire's text beside its validated source. The logic, conditions and answer codes stay the source's, so the verified flow never changes. Once a translation is complete, respondents pick their language on the survey's first page; their browser's language is offered first. Invitations, the survey pages and the AI-assisted channel all follow the respondent's language.
- **Local formats.** Numbers, dates and alphabetical sorting follow your language. Exports gain a semicolon-separated CSV for spreadsheet programs that use a decimal comma.
- **Clearer analysis messages.** Quality readings, warnings about survey answers and the reasons a dataset cannot be built or exported now read in your language, as whole sentences.

## Fixes & improvements

- List headers count correctly in every language ("1 survey", "3 surveys").
- A survey answered with data the engine could not process records a clear warning, and never keeps a copy of what the respondent typed.
- The diagram in the questionnaire editor always follows the questionnaire you are looking at.
- Signing out now ends that browser's session completely; your other devices stay signed in. Session security around sign-out and organization switching has been strengthened. No action is needed on your part.
