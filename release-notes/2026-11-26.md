# Askalot Platform Release — 2026-11-26

A questionnaire now states what each question measures, and every template starts a research project, not just a survey.

## Highlights

- **What each question measures.** Every questionnaire can carry an item inventory beside its questions: for each question, the construct it measures, what kind of answer it is (an opinion, a behaviour, a fact, knowledge…), the KPIs of your Research Paper it serves, and whether it is reverse-worded. The Designer keeps it up to date as you work, the editor shows it in an Inventory tab, and warnings point out questions that serve no goal and goals that no question serves.
- **The paper and the questionnaire stay linked.** The Research Paper's instrument section now lists, for each KPI, the questions that answer it, computed from the inventory, so it never goes out of date. The answerability check reads these links directly.
- **Analysis knows what it is analysing.** When a dataset is extracted, it records what each question measures. The Gold page proposes scale scores from questions that share a construct, KPI results are computed from the questions linked to them, and exports carry each column's construct, kind, KPIs and reverse-wording. If the inventory changes after extraction, the dataset tells you so.
- **Every template is a research starting point.** All templates in the library, in English and Hungarian, now come with a reviewed inventory and a research seed: a short motivation, two to four research questions and the KPIs that answer them. **Start a project** from a template and your Research Paper begins from that seed, with the questions already linked to its KPIs. **Start a campaign** creates the project and opens the campaign form with it filled in.
- **Rating scales, simplified.** A rating question now declares its scale by structure only: whether it runs from negative to positive or from none to most, how many points it has, and the order it is shown in. You write every label yourself, in any language. Answers like "Don't know" sit outside the scale and are never scored as a point of it.

## Fixes & improvements

- The template library was reviewed question by question: scales run consistently from low to high with fully worded labels, overlapping answer ranges were fixed, and lists end with "Something else" where an answer may be missing.
- Templates built on a validated screening instrument (for example PHQ-9 or GAD-7) use its published items and scoring, and close with a note that the result is not a diagnosis. Their Hungarian versions say that the wording is not the validated Hungarian adaptation.
- Employee surveys promise confidential, group-level reporting instead of anonymity, and questions where people rate their own manager offer "Prefer not to say".
- Templates that need your own names (your brand, competitors, session titles) mark them in brackets, so they are easy to replace before fielding.
- Some templates moved to a category that fits them better.
- The knowledge base explains the three layers of a questionnaire design (goals, inventory, questions) and how starting from a template works, in all five languages.
