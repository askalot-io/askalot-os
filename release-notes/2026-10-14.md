# Askalot Platform Release — 2026-10-14

The browser editor that arrived as a preview yesterday is now Armiger's one
editor for questionnaires, and it works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

## Highlights

- **One editor for questionnaires.** Choosing a project in Armiger, or **Design in Armiger** on a questionnaire in Targetor, opens the project in the editor that runs in your browser. It opens at once, with nothing to start or wait for. It has the questionnaire with its flow diagram, the console messages and error list, the Designer chat, the project's documents, the item preview and the Research Paper. The previous editor and its **Open in the new editor (preview)** link are gone. Your questionnaires, their version history and your documents are unchanged.

- **Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.** The editor works in Chrome and Edge 125 or newer, Firefox 147 or newer and Safari 26 or newer, on a desktop or laptop. An older browser shows a notice naming these versions instead of an empty editor.

- **Create a questionnaire from the file list.** **New File** in the `questionnaires` folder creates a questionnaire in the project and opens it, starting from the same template as a questionnaire created anywhere else. Rename and delete questionnaires from the project panel.

## Fixes & improvements

- **The keyboard stays in the text editor** when you bring a questionnaire tab to the front, when the flow diagram is open beside it, and when you show the sidebar.
- **When you and someone else save at the same time**, you choose with **Compare**, **Keep mine** or **Take theirs**. A save can no longer land on a questionnaire that was moved to another project after you opened it.
- **The Research Paper is in front** when you open a project without naming a questionnaire, in projects with many questionnaires too, and it now displays in Safari.
- **The item preview displays in Safari.**
- **A Designer message that could not be delivered** now says so in the chat and is put back in the message box, instead of waiting without an answer.

## Good to know

- The editor has no terminal, source-control view or extension marketplace.
- In the public demo, and in any organization sealed against changes, the editor opens read-only.
