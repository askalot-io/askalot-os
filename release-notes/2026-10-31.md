# Askalot Platform Release — 2026-10-31

A logic check that refuses fewer valid questionnaires, Gold column editing on a hand-picked set, and a progress answer from your AI-assisted interview.

## Highlights

- **The logic check refuses fewer valid questionnaires.** Some valid questionnaires were refused at publish, and these now publish:
  - a variable set by a later question that is asked first;
  - code attached to a free-text question.

  When a counter depends on an answer the respondent may leave blank, the check now says honestly that the questions after it are only sometimes asked, as they are when the survey runs. The knowledge base shows how to keep such a block always asked.

- **Edit Gold columns as a set.** In Balansor's Gold workbench you can tick several columns and serve, unserve, remove or move them together, and one undo reverses the whole change.

- **"How far am I?" in the AI-assisted interview.** A respondent's AI assistant can now say roughly where they are in the survey, using the same position the survey page shows.

## Fixes & improvements

- **One synthetic fill per campaign at a time.** Starting a second fill while one is running is refused with a pointer to the running one, instead of the two overwriting each other's answers.
- **Pools stay inside their Project.** Moving or merging a pool can no longer leave it holding respondents from another Project. A respondent's "Add to Pool" list offers only the pools they fit, and the pool preview says how many eligible respondents are in the pool's Project.
- **Sampling strategies** accept only the quality metrics Askalot can compute. An unknown metric is refused when you save the strategy, rather than failing later when a pool is drawn.
- **Designer:** an approval card no longer appears after you interrupt a turn, and Undo no longer removes a questionnaire that the turn moved into the project.
- **Declining consent** in the AI-assisted interview now always ends the assistant's access.
- **Faster, steadier respondent tools:** a slow internal message bus no longer holds up every connected respondent.
- **Each Askalot app now shows its own shield** in the browser tab.
