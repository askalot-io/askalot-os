# Askalot Platform Release — 2026-10-29

A new look across every Askalot app, and answer-based targets that now live
with your analysis instead of your sampling.

## Highlights

- **One design across Askalot.** Roundtable, Targetor, SirWay, Balansor and Armiger now share the look of askalot.io. Each app has its own colour on its mark, its active tab and the band at the top of the page. Actions use one consistent blue, and statuses keep the familiar red, amber and green. Lists, cards, dialogs and charts follow one set of rules in every app, and the sign-in pages match the website. The questionnaire editor has a matching theme.

- **Calmer survey pages for respondents.** The pages respondents see when they take a survey are plain and quiet, with nothing that competes with the questions.

- **Answer-based targets belong to the Bundle.** A target on a survey answer (for example, home ownership) is now a Calibration Target on the Bundle, not a factor on a Sampling Strategy. Each Calibration Target is either *raked* (weighted on) or *benchmark only* (graded, never weighted on). Bundle quality grades benchmarks on both the fielded and the weighted sample. A Sampling Strategy now holds only respondent attributes, and those alone drive the draw and the pool's quality score. Existing answer-based factors were moved onto the Bundles that used them as benchmarks.

- **Copy targets from another Bundle.** Calibration Targets can be copied from another Bundle. The targets that fit are added, the rest are listed with the reason, and nothing is saved until you click Save. An answer code that the questionnaire does not have is refused on save, with the column and the codes named.

## Fixes & improvements

- **Clearer quality reading.** The weighted Strategy grade is now called *Weighted fit*. It states how many Strategy factors it covers, and a short explainer says why a raked factor reads close to 100%. Target and sample bars overlap in a true colour mix, so both stay readable.
- **Pool quality breakdown.** A respondent pool's page shows its quality per category, target against pool, without recalculating.
- **Strategy pages** explain each quality metric in place.
- **MCP tools:** `advance_sampling_strategy` is removed. `set_calibration_targets` takes a `role` (raked or benchmark only), and the new `preview_calibration_targets_copy` previews a copy between Bundles.
