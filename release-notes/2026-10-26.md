# Askalot Platform Release — 2026-10-26

Start a questionnaire from a ready-made template, browse the whole template
library before you sign up, and get sharper, more honest verification of your
questionnaire's logic.

## Highlights

- **836 questionnaire templates, ready to use.** A library of verified templates in 13 categories (customer feedback, course evaluation, HR, healthcare, events, market research and more) is now part of Askalot. Search by any words in a template's name or description, preview its questions and its flow diagram, and create your own copy in any Project you can edit. You can start from a template on the Templates page, from "New Questionnaire from Template…" in the editor, or through the Askalot MCP tools. The copy is yours to change.

- **Browse the templates without an account.** The whole library is now public at [askalot.io/templates](https://askalot.io/templates/), with the same search, previews and flow diagrams. A free account is all it takes to use one.

- **Verification that says what it checked.** The logic checker now treats skipped and blank answers the same way the live survey does. It understands Range answers by their two ends (`.low` / `.high`), and it checks answers inside question groups and matrices. Where a condition can only be checked approximately, the editor now shows a warning instead of staying silent. QML arithmetic is whole numbers, and number fields accept only whole numbers, so what is checked is exactly what respondents can enter.

- **A new askalot.io.** The website has a new look and a shorter homepage, and it states plainly what verification does and does not decide.

## Fixes & improvements

- **Organization announcements.** Organization admins can post a message to their members, shown on every page. Planned maintenance is announced ahead of time, and fair-use notices are shown only to signed-in people, never on respondent pages.
- **Response quality** shows a standard deviation per question instead of a raw variance.
- **Answers are checked against their question.** An answer outside its question's allowed values, or to a question that is not shown, is refused instead of stored.
- **Deleting an organization** now also removes its files and indexed documents.
- The "Test a questionnaire" list shows the right date for questionnaires that were never edited.
