# Askalot Platform Release — 2026-10-06

This release makes the Askalot documentation directly usable by AI assistants
and agents, and fixes several things in sign-in records, the audit trail and
AI summaries.

## Highlights

- **The documentation is available as Markdown.** Every page of docs.askalot.io has a Markdown version: add `index.md` to the page address, or request the page with `Accept: text/markdown`. An index of all pages is at `https://docs.askalot.io/llms.txt`, and the whole documentation in one file at `https://docs.askalot.io/llms-full.txt`.

- **AI assistants may read and cite Askalot's public pages.** askalot.io and docs.askalot.io now state explicitly that search engines and AI tools may index, quote and learn from them. Your organization's workspace, surveys and data are not public pages and are unaffected.

## Fixes & improvements

- **The audit trail shows every level you are entitled to.** The viewer lists the same events the audit API returns for your role.
- **Google and Microsoft sign-ins are recorded as logins.** A sign-in through either provider now appears in the audit trail like a password sign-in, with the provider named.
- **Escape no longer leaves the page.** Pressing Escape closes the sign-in dialog when it is open and otherwise does nothing.
- **Clearer messages when an AI summary cannot run.** A document summary that is waiting on your AI setup, or on a sign-in to your AI provider that has expired, now says so and tells you what to do.
- **The Survey Presentation Format page** shows its description correctly in search results and link previews.
