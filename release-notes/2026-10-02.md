# Askalot Platform Release — 2026-10-02

This release separates who your sample should look like from how many people
you draw. A sampling strategy now describes the target population and how fit
is graded, and every pool you draw from it records its own size and selection
settings. Alongside that, surveys record only answers respondents actually
give, open-text answers can be classified and coded per analysis, and
organisations share exactly one thing: the respondent panel.

## Highlights

- **One sampling strategy, pools of any size.** Choose the size, oversampling, selection method, email requirement and any respondents to exclude each time you draw a pool. A 200-person pilot and a 1,000-person main wave can come from the same strategy, and both are graded against the same targets. Each pool shows the settings it was drawn with next to its actual member count, and refreshing a pool redraws with its own settings, never resizing it to match a later change elsewhere. Existing pools keep the settings their strategy had when this release arrived.

- **Surveys record only answers respondents give.** A question's default is now a visual suggestion only. Moving past a question without touching it no longer stores the default, a switch's "off", or a slider's starting position as if the respondent had chosen it.

- **Classify and code open-text answers per analysis.** In a study, mark each open-text column as descriptive, a category, or an identifier. Category answers are coded by counting recurring responses; identifier columns are never coded or shown in summaries.

- **Pool quality reflects the people as they were drawn.** Representativeness is graded against each member's attributes at the moment they joined the pool, so later edits to a respondent's profile no longer shift the quality score of a sample already in the field.

- **Clear rules for what an organisation shares.** Projects, questionnaires, campaigns, surveys, studies and datasets belong to their owners in every organisation. A private organisation shares its respondent panel (respondents, pools, strategies and reference documents) with every member, and nothing else. Lists and pickers show only the projects you can open.

## Fixes & improvements

- **Changing organisation is in one place.** Every sign-in opens your home organisation; switching organisation shows which one is your primary and which is current.
- **Starting a campaign for an agent** now asks how many respondents to draw, rather than assuming 100.
- **The formal-verification paper behind questionnaire checking** is cited by its DOI on askalot.io and in the knowledge base.
