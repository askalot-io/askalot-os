# Askalot Platform Release — 2026-11-11

Bring your existing questionnaires into Askalot, in any language they were written in.

## Highlights

- **Import a questionnaire from PDF or Markdown.** On the questionnaire design page, open a project (or create one on the spot) and import an existing questionnaire. Askalot reads the questions, answer options, grids and routing, builds a verified questionnaire from them, and keeps the source beside it, so you can always compare against what you started from. Parts that are hard to read, such as dense grids or unusual answer marks, are read more carefully instead of being guessed.
- **Questionnaires in any language.** A questionnaire can now be written in any language, not only the five the product itself speaks. Choose "Other language…" in the questionnaire editor and enter its language code (for example `it` or `pt-BR`). Respondents see your questions as written, with the survey pages around them in a supported language. Checks that depend on a word list, such as spotting leading wording, say "not judged" for such a questionnaire instead of reporting nothing.
- **Sharper logic checking.** Conditions that compare free-text answers are now treated as undecided rather than proven false, and the editor shows where a condition could not be decided. A questionnaire that passes validation behaves exactly as checked.

## Fixes & improvements

- Profile and organization settings are grouped into sections that show a summary when collapsed.
- Filter panels on list pages start closed, and open by themselves while a filter is applied.
- The questionnaire editor validates one change at a time and redraws only the part of the diagram that changed, so large questionnaires stay responsive; its outline now covers the largest imported questionnaires.
- Simulated answers to a question about someone's "current state" (of a project, a process, a feeling) are no longer a place name.
- **Action needed if you used a Claude subscription as an AI provider:** that option has been removed. Open your AI model settings and choose another provider for any slot that showed it.
