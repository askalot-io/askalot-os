# Askalot Platform Release — 2026-10-03

Respondents can now take a survey inside their own AI assistant. The assistant
plays the interviewer: it introduces the survey and explains a question when
asked, and it never answers. This sits beside the two ways a survey could
already be taken, on the web through a link and with a human interviewer.

## Highlights

- **AI-assisted interviews.** When you create a campaign, choose whether to also offer the AI assistant, and write the introduction and consent text respondents will see there. A respondent who picks it gets a connector address and a pairing code on their survey page, adds the connector to their assistant (Claude, for example), and starts a chat. The survey appears as a panel in the chat, with the same questions, controls, skip logic and Back and Next buttons as the web survey. Every answer is the respondent's own click in that panel. The assistant cannot record, change or skip an answer, and nothing typed in the chat is recorded.

- **Consent that names the AI provider.** Before the first question, the respondent sees your introduction and consent text, followed by a fixed paragraph explaining that questions and answers pass through their assistant's provider. A respondent who declines is disconnected and can carry on with the web link. The assistant learns the respondent's answers only after they consent.

- **Respondents stay in control of the connection.** A pairing code can connect up to three assistants. Getting a new code disconnects every assistant connected with the old one. A respondent who leaves can come back, even in a new chat, and continues from their current question with their answers kept. Finishing the survey ends the connection.

- **Compare channels in your data.** Each survey records how it was taken: directly on the web, with an interviewer, or AI-assisted. Datasets carry that as a `_mode` column, and data quality now reports completion by mode: of the people who started in each mode, how many completed, partly completed or broke off, with a confidence interval once a mode has at least 30 starters.

- **Preview a question from your own AI assistant.** If you work with Askalot through an assistant, you can ask it to show one questionnaire item as respondents will see it, rendered in the chat.

## Fixes & improvements

- **Two windows on one survey no longer overwrite each other.** If a survey is open in two places and one of them is out of date, it now says the survey changed elsewhere and asks you to reload, instead of saving over the newer answers.
- **Back behaves predictably.** Back on the first question leaves you there, and stepping back into a repeated section shows which repetition you are on.
- **The strategy list shows how many pools** were drawn from each sampling strategy.
- **Drawing a pool through an assistant or the API** now considers the same respondents you can see in the app.

## If you use the API or an assistant to create campaigns

Creating a campaign now requires you to state whether it offers the AI assistant (`offers_ai_channel`: `true` or `false`). There is no default, and a request that leaves it out is refused. When it is `true`, the introduction and consent text are required too.

Existing datasets gain the `_mode` column the next time their data is extracted.
