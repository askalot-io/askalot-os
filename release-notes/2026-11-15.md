# Askalot Platform Release — 2026-11-15

Read your Research Paper in your own language, and size your sample from your research goals.

## Highlights

- **The Research Paper in your language.** A paper now carries a translation per language beside its original text. Readers see the paper, its figures and its headings in their own language, and can switch to another language on the page. Each section shows whether its translation is current or behind the original, and "changed since you read it" is tracked separately for each language.
- **Size your sample from your goals.** Askalot plans how many respondents to draw from the goals written in your paper, and the Generate page offers that size when you build a pool.
- **Templates in Hungarian.** The template library, and the public template catalog, are now available in Hungarian. A template you copy comes in your language when the library has it, with its other languages kept as translations.
- **A new documentation site.** docs.askalot.io has a new look that matches askalot.io, with faster built-in search, diagrams that load instantly, and shorter pages that end with a link to the previous and the next page.

## Fixes & improvements

- The questionnaire editor, including its diagram and panels, now follows your language. The Hungarian interface reads in one consistent, formal voice.
- A failed analysis step now explains what went wrong in a sentence in your language, instead of showing a raw error code.
- An expired session takes you to the one sign-in screen, and "Forgot your password?" sends you to the place that holds your account.
- A respondent can have only one live survey per campaign; inviting or filling again resumes or skips the existing one instead of creating a duplicate.
- Simulated mass fills skip any survey a real respondent has already reached, and never mark it as invited.
- Logic checking is more precise for answers that a questionnaire's code writes itself, including grid cells and question groups.
