# Askalot Platform Release — 2026-10-30

A smoother path from a template to a fielded survey, a logic check that accepts more of the questionnaires you write, and synthetic answers straight from a campaign.

## Highlights

- **From template to campaign in fewer steps.** You can preview a template before creating anything from it. Starting a campaign from a template now preselects its questionnaire on the campaign form. Roundtable's home page leads with these steps, and its service cards say plainly what each app is for.

- **The logic check accepts more valid questionnaires.** Several questionnaires that run correctly were refused when you published them. These include an item checked against its own answer, group and matrix checks, conditional expressions, and code that sets a variable on only one branch. They now publish. A block that counts answers and stops early ("after the third *No*, skip the rest of this block") is accepted, and its stopping point is verified item by item.

- **Fill a campaign with synthetic answers from its page.** A campaign's page in Targetor can fill its pool's surveys with synthetic answers, drawn at random or realistically. The fill needs no AI provider, the answers are marked synthetic, and the page follows the run until it finishes. A campaign that has already sent real invitations can't be filled.

## Fixes & improvements

- **Long conditions wrap** in the questionnaire diagram instead of running off the edge.
- **Respondent pages** list only the campaigns a respondent is actually in, matching the count above them, and the respondent phone field has been removed.
- **Pools stay within their Project.** Generating, previewing and refreshing a pool draws from the pool's own Project (or from the whole shared panel in a private organization). Adding a respondent from another Project to a pool is refused with an explanation.
- **Designer guidance** now gates a branch of questions once at the block instead of repeating the same condition on every item. The knowledge base explains the three things a block does: grouping, branching and rosters.
- **Designer:** Undo and approving a change are more reliable, and several questionnaire save and link issues are fixed.
- **MCP tools:** `mass_fill_surveys` no longer accepts the `stratified` mode. Use `realistic` or `random`.
