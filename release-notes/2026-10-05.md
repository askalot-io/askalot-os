# Askalot Platform Release — 2026-10-05

This release changes two things in your datasets: completeness now counts
questions a respondent was shown and left unanswered, and the columns that carry
respondent attributes have new names. It also tidies where AI settings live.

## Highlights

- **Completeness sees abandonment.** A survey's completeness is now the share of the questions the respondent *reached* that they answered, including the question they were on when they left. A survey abandoned after one answer no longer scores as fully complete. Datasets carry the number of questions reached in a `_reached` column.

- **Only the respondent attributes you use reach a dataset.** A custom attribute is written into a dataset only when a sampling strategy or a calibration target of that dataset names it. Other imported contact data stays with the respondent record and is no longer copied into dataset files or exports. The calibration targets page lists the attributes your surveyed respondents have, shows which ones the dataset already holds, and tells you when a re-extract is needed.

- **Organization-wide AI defaults.** In a private organization, the organization's administrator can choose one AI provider and a model for each of the three tiers under **Organization → AI defaults**. Members who have set nothing of their own use those. In a shared organization each member keeps their own settings, and the page says so.

- **AI status in the account menu.** The account menu has a new **AI Status** item showing, for each AI capability, whether it is configured and where the setting comes from. The separate AI indicator in the navigation bar is gone.

## Fixes & improvements

- **"Not configured" means the same thing everywhere.** An AI tier with no model selected is now reported as not configured on every screen, instead of appearing ready on some.
- **Simulated responses use your own AI provider.** Filling surveys with AI-generated answers runs on the provider configured for you or your organization. If none is configured, the run stops before the first survey and says what to set.
- **The organization AI settings page** saves and reloads reliably.
- **The audit trail follows your role.** A tenant administrator sees events across the whole tenant. An organization administrator or manager sees the events of the organization they are working in. Events recorded without an organization, such as sign-ins, appear in the tenant-wide view.

## If you work with dataset columns

Two column prefixes changed. If you reference these columns in exports, scripts or through the API, update the names:

| Before | Now |
|---|---|
| `_demo_<field>` | `_demographic_<field>` |
| `_attr_<key>` | `_custom_<key>` |

Calibration targets and Gold operations you had already saved were updated to the new names automatically.

Existing datasets need to be extracted again before they can be derived or graded. Until then they report that their extraction is out of date. Re-extracting also applies the new completeness and drops custom-attribute columns that nothing names.
