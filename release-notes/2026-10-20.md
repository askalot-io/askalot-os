# Askalot Platform Release — 2026-10-20

A short release: it prepares fair-use limits for the free service, and makes
paid features more dependable.

## Highlights

- **Fair-use limits for the free service are on the way.** Every feature of Askalot stays free. To keep the free service equally fast for everyone, limits will apply per person on the number of Projects, collaborators per Project, active or paused campaigns, and completed responses each month. They are not switched on yet. The start date will be announced in the product at least 30 days ahead, and anyone already at a limit then will also get an email. Paid plans are not affected.

- **Nothing you have is ever taken away.** When the limits apply, they only stop something *new* from starting past a limit. Existing Projects stay, running campaigns keep collecting, and every respondent is recorded. Before and after the start date, your profile shows each count against its limit, and a notice appears as you approach one.

Details: [Fair-Use Limits](https://docs.askalot.io/guide/fair-use/).

## Fixes & improvements

- **Paid features no longer depend on the billing system being reachable.** The features your plan includes are now checked inside your own Askalot service, so a brief interruption on the billing side no longer shows them as temporarily unavailable. Nothing changes in what your plan includes.
