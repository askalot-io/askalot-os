# Askalot Platform Release — 2026-11-17

The Size Planner gets its own page, and the documentation speaks your language.

## Highlights

- **A Size Planner page of its own.** Plan how many respondents to invite from the Size Planner entry in the top menu, from your Project, or from your Research Paper. The page opens on a plan proposed from the goals and rates written in your paper, and shows for each value whether it came from the paper or is a default you should check. Sliders for margin of error, confidence, response and eligibility rates, and design effect update the result and the chart as you move them, and the chart shows how many completes you need and how many people to invite to reach them. Saving the plan writes it to your sampling strategy and to the paper's "Sample size" section.
- **Documentation in five languages.** docs.askalot.io is now available in English, German, Spanish, French and Hungarian.

## Fixes & improvements

- German, Spanish and French wording in campaign management, the questionnaire designer and the API now uses one term for each concept.
