# Askalot Platform Release — 2026-11-01

Contact us from askalot.io itself, with replies straight back to you.

## Highlights

- **New: a contact form at askalot.io/contact.** Every "Talk to us" and "Request Private Org" button on askalot.io now opens a short form instead of your mail client, with the topic already chosen. It works on phones and on computers without a configured mail app.
- **Replies come straight back to you.** Your message reaches our team at info@askalot.io, and our reply goes to the work email you entered.
- **Nothing is stored.** Your message goes only to our mailbox — no database, no CRM. The form will not send without the consent box ticked, and it checks that on our side, not just in your browser.
- **Prefer email?** info@askalot.io is still shown on the page, including when JavaScript is off.
